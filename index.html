Scene 01 · Origins

It started with a small keyboard

I grew up in Mudavantheri, a village in Kerala, the youngest of four. A small keyboard at home was my first playground, long before I knew words like production or composition.

School stages taught me courage. I won at district and state school arts festivals in Mappilapattu, Arabic vocal, drama, mimicry and mono-act. By higher secondary I was performing concerts often enough that the fees paid for my college and hostel.

At Farook College I learned to see a story as a complete experience: sound, image, rhythm and pacing. I've been chasing that ever since.
